# Tiny Town pilot — Day 2

Source: Kenney Tiny Town 1.1, https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-town, CC0 (see License.txt). Original 16×16 PNG bytes retained; SHA-256 recorded in ground_truth.csv. K identifiers encode original file index. This supersedes Day 1 for future experiments; no crosswalk is asserted.

30 purposively selected tiles, seven mutually exclusive primary visual roles. Dirt includes grass/dirt transition tiles; vegetation includes tree components; roof includes gable tiles; door includes surrounding wall. These are operational benchmark conventions, not official Kenney semantic labels. Labels are preliminary visual annotations checked against the atlas and sample scene, awaiting independent human review. Do not provide ground_truth.csv to models. Use numbered_tiles.png for both conditions and Sample.png only for the example condition. Selection is not representative of the entire 132-tile pack.
